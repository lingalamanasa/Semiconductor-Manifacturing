FAQ

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Answers to common questions regarding NDA signing, PDK downloads, and shuttle tapeout requirements.

What EDA design suites are supported for PDK v2.4 verification? +
We support Synopsys Custom Compiler, Cadence Virtuoso, and Siemens EDA Calibre PDK rule decks. Full DRC, LVS, and PEX decks are included in your client engineering dashboard.
How do I sign a mutual NDA to obtain PDK access? +
When you register for an account on our Registration Portal, our legal team automatically sends an e-sign corporate NDA. Once executed, full PDK files are unlocked instantly.
What is the lead time from GDSII file lock to packaged wafer delivery? +
Standard CyberShuttle MPW turnaround time is 45 business days from GDSII lock to tested & dicing-ready wafers, including high-NA EUV lithography exposure and atomic metrology.
