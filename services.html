Client Information

Foundry & PDK Licensing FAQ

Frequently asked questions about design rule decks, shuttle runs, and PDK access.

How do I request PDK access for Sub-2nm GAA logic designs? +
Sign up for an engineering account through our Registration Portal. Upon corporate NDA verification, certified PDK v2.4 rule decks (DRC, LVS, PEX, TCAD Spice models) are instantly available in your dashboard.
What is the schedule for Multi-Project Wafer (MPW) shuttle departures? +
Stackly operates quarterly CyberShuttle MPW runs. Upcoming runs depart on August 25, September 10, and October 18. GDSII files must be locked and DRC-clean 10 days prior to shuttle departure.
Does Stackly support 3D Heterogeneous interposer stacking? +
Yes! Our 3D Bumpless Interposer technology supports sub-micron pitch die-to-die interconnects, UCIe 2.0 protocols, and vertical HBM3e high-bandwidth memory integration.
What ISO cleanroom standards are maintained in your facilities? +
All main fab facilities in Santa Clara, Dresden, and Hsinchu are ISO Class-1 certified with active particle count under 0.01 particles per cubic meter and strict ambient temperature control.
